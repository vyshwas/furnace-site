What you get back

Every check ends in one of four answers.

  • Proven

    It works. Checked in a real browser.

  • Repaired

    Fixed, then re-checked by a separate verifier.

  • Needs you

    A judgment call. Flagged, never guessed.

  • Unknown

    Can't prove it? Then it's not a pass.

How it works

Three steps. No setup.

  1. 01

    Install

    One line. Stays on your machine.

    $ ./install.sh
  2. 02

    Point it at your app

    It reads every route and page.

    $ node furnace/bin/furnace.js launch-check --workspace=./my-app
  3. 03

    Get a straight answer

    READY, NOT READY, or NOT SURE YET. Each problem comes with a fix you can paste into your AI assistant.

The details

Honest about its limits.

What it doesn't do
  • Page checks need your app running. Furnace doesn't start it for you.
  • Fixes are verified on the changed file, not by re-rendering the page.
  • Login screens and payments need a human. Flagged, never guessed.
  • Gradients and live keyboard behaviour come back unknown, not passing.
  • Receipt fingerprints show edits. They are not signatures.
  • Tested on Windows for this release. Linux last tested on v0.1.0. macOS not tested.
  • Code-reading checks look for patterns. Anything they can't establish is a question for you.
What else it checks
  • Login and ownership checks on every server route, admin limits, rate limits on paid AI or email calls.
  • Supabase row-level security, Firebase rules, secrets in code and git history.
  • Your live site with --url: headers, cookies, CORS, exposed files.
  • furnace blocks --since=HEAD reviews only what your AI assistant just changed.
  • Compliance signals: third-party fonts, trackers before consent, unsubscribe links, renewal terms, report buttons. Signals only — not legal advice.
